Corporate Tax and Trade

Sovos acquires Blue dot, adding AI-powered VAT recovery capabilities across travel & expense and accounts payable to expand its tax compliance platform

Tradeshift establishes Tradeshift Arabia in Saudi Arabia to offer e-invoicing, procure-to-pay automation and supplier collaboration solutions to local organisations

 

Workiva launches Agent Studio and new AI capabilities to automate regulatory reporting, internal audit and GRC workflows, reducing manual work across regulatory processes

 

Numeral expands integration ecosystem across SAP S/4HANA, Zoho Billing, Intuit Enterprise Suite, Solidus and Measure

 

Zoho Books launches a Nigeria edition with local VAT, withholding tax and e-invoicing capabilities to support businesses with Nigerian tax compliance

 

Gaia Dynamics raises $7M to expand its AI platform for tariff management, customs compliance and trade risk, helping businesses respond to changing global trade requirements

Checkbox launches First Pass, a context-aware AI agent that reviews contracts, invoices, and incoming legal requests, scoring them against a team's own playbook before they reach a lawyer

 

Wolters Kluwer launches an agentic AI tool for outside counsel management, letting legal operations teams compare law firms on cost, staffing, and billing compliance
